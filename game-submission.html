FOR GAME CREATORS

Have a game to share?

We’re interested in original, polished browser games from developers around the world.

What we look for

  • Games that run smoothly in modern mobile and desktop browsers.
  • Clear touch, keyboard or mouse controls.
  • Fast loading, accessible interface and original artwork.
  • Documented ownership and permission for distribution and monetization.
  • No hidden downloads, deceptive UI or unapproved tracking.

How to submit

Prepare a playable URL, developer details, a short description, supported devices and the exact license or written distribution permission. Then contact us with those details.
