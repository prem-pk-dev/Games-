A LITTLE ABOUT US

Play should be simple.

We’re building a welcoming place to discover quick, delightful browser games.

A world of little games and big breaks.

Games is an independent browser-gaming project designed around one simple idea: getting to a fun game should be easy. Browse by mood, discover something new, and play supported games right in your browser.

Made for more screens

The experience is designed mobile-first, with responsive layouts for phones, tablets, laptops and desktops. We aim to keep navigation clear, pages lightweight and game controls easy to understand.

Growing carefully

Our catalogue includes original mini-games and game concepts under development. Third-party games will only be added when their source, license and distribution permissions have been checked.
