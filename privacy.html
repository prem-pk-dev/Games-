Privacy notice

Plain-language information about this early static website.

Local browser storage

This prototype may use your browser’s localStorage to remember favorites, recently played games, theme preference and local game scores. This information stays in the browser on your device unless you clear site data.

This version does not provide user accounts or a server-side profile. Your local favorites and history do not sync across devices.

Future versions may include licensed third-party games, analytics or advertising. Any such services must be disclosed here before they are enabled.

Contact
